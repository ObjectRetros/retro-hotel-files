# SnowStorm arena assets

The PNG sprites under:

- `flash/c_images/snowstorm_client/arena_props`
- `nitro/nitro-assets/images/snowstorm_client/arena_props`

are the original SnowStorm room bitmaps extracted from
`Quackster/v26_assets` at commit
`f7b1d275e4ba98aee60df794bbbeb43a2ccd43bf`, directory
`hh_game_snowwar_room/bitmaps`.

They cover the four classic trees and their shadows, the snowman, snow and ice
blocks, both fence directions, and all six visible snowball-machine states.

The archive exported these bitmaps against an opaque white matte. Only the
border-connected pure-white matte pixels were made transparent; enclosed white
pixels belonging to the snow artwork remain unchanged.
